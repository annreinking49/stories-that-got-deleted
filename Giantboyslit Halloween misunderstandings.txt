Cole has empathy for his food, so his dad teaches him that all food wants to be eaten... which backfires when he shrinks before his son. All food secretly wants to be eaten even when it begs for mercy, after all.

"B-but they look sad!" Cole protested, tears in his eyes, brandishing one of the gingerbread men he refused to eat. Sure enough, the neighbor had done a crap job of piping the faces, they all looked like they were screaming in agony. Chris rubbed the bridge of his nose, trying to find his patience—he needed to, as the single father of a 7yo boy.

"Everything in your pumpkin is food," Chris explained in his best "dad" voice, as he pointed to the pumpkin-shaped bag filled with sweets. "Do you want to know a big secret? Food actually wants to be eaten. Food loves it."

Cole sniffled sadly. "Even... even if it's begging and saying 'no please don't eat me'...?"

"Especially when it's begging you not to eat it," Chris insisted, taking on a conspirational tone. "Pretending to not love it is part of the fun, that's why you have to eat them even when they are all like..." And Chris grabbed one gingerbread man under the armpits, shaking it left and right, before continuing in a fake high-pitched voice, "Oh no, mister giant sir, please don't eat meeee, I am so small and so delicious aaaa!" Chris and Cole laughed together, and to Chris' relief, his son started shoving a gingerbread man into his mouth enthusiastically. He ruffled the boy's mop of blond hair and got up with a sigh.

He started working on cooking the stew for dinner, with the occasional glance at the boy wearing a kaiju pajama and doing coloring while feasting on his Halloween bounty of candy on the kitchen table. He lowered the fire after a while, while Cole was in the bathroom, and he was starting to walk towards the drawings his son left half-colored on the table when he felt a sudden, overwhelming weakness and felt himself trip and fall. The last thing he saw was his kitchen table rapidly advancing towards him as he fell onto it.

When he woke up, he was maybe three inches tall, and his son's pumpkin bag towered over him like a building, while the candy wrappers abandoned across the table were as tall if not taller than Chris. He felt his chest contract with horror and anxiety as he realized that he'd contracted the shrinking virus. Fuck.

His eyes traveled across the tabletop, looking for the best place to get noticed. He could stand atop the drawing Cole was working on, he thought, before seeing that the page was covered in wrappers coated in slowly-melting chocolate. Chris did NOT want to know how Cole would react to seeing him stained with chocolate after he cleared the area. His son was still at that age where he was kept strictly away from shrinkees because he could not be trusted with them.

Shoving his anxiety aside, Chris started scaling the side of the plastic pumpkin bag. He needed to be seen, then to convince Cole to call 911. That was his only chance.

Standing on the rim of the bag, he looked inside, grimacing at the landscape of sweet treats, all bigger than himself. The ground suddenly shook under his feet—Cole was walking out of the bathroom! Each step his son took made the whole world vibrate for his tiny dad... until Chris lost his balance and collapsed inside. Thankfully, the piles of candy broke his fall.

Outside, he heard a chair groan while being dragged, and an unfathomable immense weight collapse onto a cushion—Cole had sat down. "COLE!" Chris screamed through the bag's opening. "COLE, IN HERE!"

Thankfully, his voice was loud enough that his son grabbed the pumpkin bag right away. Cole tilted the bag, making Chris lose his balance and fall face-first into the candies, but still... "DAD?!" Chris could have cried of relief when he saw his son's adorable face, framed by his godzilla hood, appear in the opening of the bag, staring straight at him.

"Get me out of here, Cole," Chris ordered, and Cole complied. The sight of a hand, larger than several men like him, coming straight for him, finger splayed, awoke a primal fear in Chris, which he forced himself to shut up. He could trust his son. The fingers wrapped around him and yanked him out—he expected Cole to drop him on the table, but instead, his son kept holding him, staring at him with wide, disbelieving eyes.

"Dad...? Wha-what happened...?" Cole asked.

"I need you to call 911, son," Chris replied. "Remember how we talked about 911? You know your numbers, I know you can do it," he insisted when he saw that Cole seemed torn, looking back and forth between his shrunken father and something beyond him then biting his lower lip. "What's wrong? I know you know how the phone works."

"It's... it's not that..." Cole said weakly, looking distraught. He wiped his mouth with the back of his hand, leaving a trail of drool on his skin.

"... Cole? What's wrong...?" Chris asked again, feeling dread rising in his chest.

"I-I don't want to eatcha, dad..."

"Eat... waitwaitwait, you must not eat me!" Chris instructed in a shrill voice, panic leaking through his tone. That did not calm Cole down at all.

"I-I know that pretending you don't like it is part of the fun, bu-but can you not beg? It makes me feel sad," Cole sighed, looking genuinely devastated. Chris' blood ran cold.

"No-no, wait, actually wait- I need you to understand-" He begged, feeling his whole body tremble. Suddenly, he was hyper-aware of the fingers wrapped around the torso, waist and legs, leaving only his head and shoulders free. Cole's skin was velvetty, warm, even too warm, using powerful compressive power on him as casually as if he were holding one of his crayons. Ignoring him, the giant boy raised his dad to his face. "COLE WAIT A SECOND! I'M NOT FOOD!"

"Everything in my pumpkin is food. You said it yourself," Cole repeated Chris' own words at him, his words blowing a warm breeze over his terrified dad. "That's why you crawled in, right? Ya wanna be eaten, it's proof that yer food."

"N-no... no, it's not..." Chris, somehow, couldn't even find the energy to deny. Cole had opened his mouth wide after talking, leaving his dad not even one second of respite before he stared down into the dark abyss of his son's gullet, past the glistening tongue and teeth, still stained with chocolate and peanut butter. "No.... please... don't..." His voice was so faint that even he could barely hear himself.

Cole was extraordinarily gentle when he deposited his father on the vast expanse of his tongue. The boy usually feasted and chomped down on food without a second thought, but his dad was a once-in-a-lifetime meal he did not want to mistreat. Chris would have been touched if he weren't so busy trembling violently and choking on his own panicked tears.

Behind him, the lips closed, casting him into an oppressive darkness as a glob of thick drool slowly descended onto Chris and draped itself over his face. The very air reeked of chocolate and peanut as well as a smell Chris could only describe as "profoundly Cole" scalding the back of his nostrils.

"COLE!" Chris finally managed to shout, pulling himself out of his stupor. "FOOD IS DIGESTED WHEN YOU EAT IT! I WILL DIE! SPIT ME OUT, PLEA-" He was shut up by the tongue rolling like waves underneath him, sending him sprawling and drenching him to the bone.

"Shtop beggin, daddy," Cole said through his full mouth; the words came out as solid walls of solid slamming into the shrunken man, leaving him writhing in pain and holding his burst ears. "I know ya wantchit." And just like that, the boy swallowed.

Chris' screams of terror and despair were muffled to nothing in a split second as his whole body was sucked across the mouth and down the throat, the muscles contracting around him without mercy. He traveled far, FAR down, blinded by an infinity of Cole embracing him on every side, until he plopped pathetically into a gigantic cavity churning with liquid. Chris wanted to puke when he realized where he was.

"I-I hope it was fun for ya, daddy," came muffled, yet still extremely powerful words filtered through the walls of the stomach and the boy's soft belly. "I will eat a buncha chocolate, so you can have lots of Halloween treats too! Ya can eat everything ya get down there, don't feel bad okay? I wanna share with ya because I love you daddy!"

The words were so sweet, but Chris, now perched on a half-digested gingerbread man's torso as if it were a raft, sobbed uncontrollably. Noises like impossibly large drums echoed across the stomach... was Cole slapping his belly?

Chris witnessed, helpless, as chunks of food larger than himself appeared in the opening in the ceiling and crashed into the stomach fluids, splashing Chris' face with his son's stomach acid. It tingled on his skin.

Outside, Cole had resumed drawing—or rather, struggling to slather colored crayons within the lines of his Disney coloring book, while happily munching on a gingerbread man. The boy would call 911, hours too late, but for now, he enjoyed having the night after Halloween to himself and his daddy, chronically patting his belly and talking to it, thanking his daddy for letting him stay up past his bedtime. When the hospital did an X-ray of Cole's stomach, it was deemed that the boy had eaten his father by accident, and that nothing would be done about it. The tech operating the X-ray machine elected not to tell anyone that the shrinkee was still writhing inside.
