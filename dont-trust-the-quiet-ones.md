Rory was writing on the blackboard when a paper bullet hit the back of his head. He turned around just in time to see Jeremy trying to hide the paper straw he shot it out of.

  
  

"Jeremy, that will be an hour of detention," Rory said to the little asshole.

  
  

"Fuck you, teach," Jeremy shot back with that confident half-smirk of his. He knew his dad was friends with the principal and he would never be expelled.

  
  

"Two hours, then."

  
  

Jeremy gave him the finger but didn't argue further. The way his friends smiled at him and laughed discreetly, Rory suspected that detention just made Jeremy look like a badass rebel to the other 11 year olds of the class.

  
  

Rory refrained from sighing, no matter how much he wanted to. As the teacher, he had to be the bigger man—and he quite literally towered over the bully anyway, so it wasn't as if Jeremy could really be a threat. Just a 5 foot tall annoyance with gelled hair and baggy clothes. Rory's eyes fell on the first row of desks, and he smiled genuinely. Sitting as close to the board as possible and surrounded by neatly ordered notes, Theo stared up at him with wide eyes only made wider by his thick, round glasses. The mousy boy was so short that his feet hovered over the floor, kicking mindlessly as he stimmed.

  
  

"Remind the class, Theo, what was I saying?" Rory asked his best student.

  
  

Theo gave a genuine smile, seemingly overjoyed to be called on. "You were talking about covalent bonds, sir!"

  
  

"Thank you! It's so nice to see that SOME students still care about their education," Rory said while staring squarely at Jeremy, who made a face in response. Rory turned back to the board and continued. "So, convalent bonds. What you need to understand is-"
