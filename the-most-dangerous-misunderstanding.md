# The most dangerous misunderstanding

## Summary
In a world where shrinking is commonplace, children are the scariest - because they can kill shrunken people in total impunity. A group of friends learn that lesson intimately when one of them is shrunk at home near his little brother, and they attempt to rescue him...

---

Kyle's lips were locked with Lissa's every second they did not get forced apart. Glued together, they tumbled out of the taxi and against the wall outside of Kyle's bedroom window.

"Shh, shh!" Kyle whispered, half-laughing from the booze. "My dad's like, super strict, he'd chew me out if he knew I'm bringin' ya!" He cupped his girlfriend's ass and shoved his tongue down her throat again, before pushing the window open.

The couple suddenly had something much bigger to worry about than a father's anger. The entire world grew at incredible speeds around them, until each blade of grass was nearly as tall as Kyle, taller than Lissa. A moment later, the air quaked in rhythm as a pair of flip-flops, each the size of a house, stepped closer.

Kyle held Lissa close, terrified.

"Been three weeks already, eh?" Jesse asked, staring sadly into his beer.

"Three weeks tomorrow. Never forget the day, dummy," Marlon growled. He'd been the deepest hit by the loss of Brandon, they were best friends.

"How could any of us forget? The brat got it on video," Jimmy reminded them, as if anyone needed a reminder. They all had a vivid memory of the shaky smartphone footage of a boy, maybe 10, filming himself selfie-mode while holding Brandon, shrunken to one inch tall, above his open mouth. Other kids were watching and cheering. They'd learned after the fact that this had been a dare between the kids, eating Brandon. Brandon's own little brother was in the crowd, cheering—he'd been paid $8 for his brother, and he considered it a good deal.

Marlon and Jimmy both flinched when a boy, maybe 8, walked past them.

"Guys, not all kids are dangerous, relax," Jesse commented at their reaction.

Marlon and Jimmy both glared at him, annoyed. The boy did not say or do anything, but in a post-shrinking society, there was literally nothing more terrifying than a little boy.

Shrinking technology had been spread far and wide, far too much to suppress it, before the obvious consequences were felt; shrinking could never be reversed, but getting one's hands on a shrink ray or shrinking serum was relatively easy, not much harder than buying weed, so shrunken people were _everywhere_. Someone shrinking near an adult would sometimes meet a terrible fate, but this would be considered murder by the police; kids, however, could not be tried for it, despite having every bit as much power to kill the shrunken people as anyone else. Perhaps more, given the lack of care boys that age show.

Everyone knew at least a few people who'd been shrunk somehow and were taken out by a kid, just for fun. The kids never suffered any consequences, so they never showed hesitation. Just like the boy who'd swallowed Brandon on a dare hadn't hesitated one moment despite the shrinkee's cries of mercy.

Jimmy's own older brother had died that way. He had been staying home to babysit their kid brother, the miracle kid born a dozen years after Jimmy when their mother thought she was already menopausal. He shrank while their kid brother was preparing for school, and he was put into the boy's pocket—the back pocket of his jeans. The autopsy confirmed that he'd died in the late afternoon, after an entire day at school, being sat on for hours on end during classes. Their kid brother was still the favorite of their parents.

The text that Jimmy got the next moment activated all of his fears. It was from Kyle.

  
  

_"am home help shrunk bro kill"_

  
  

Jimmy's forehead immediately felt beaded with sweat. "Hey, guys... Kyle has an 11 year old brother, right...?"

Dread spread across the group. "Did he... get shrunk...?" Marlon asked. Jimmy nodded, and all three were rushing out the next moment. There wouldn't be another Brandon.

\*\*\*

The click of the backdoor's lock shifting open under the attentions of Marlon's lockpick attracted the attention of the other two friends, who'd been keeping watch. Together, the three friends took a step, in unison, into the back entrance of Kyle's house.

  
  

Not one second later, they were all looking UP at the underside of chairs and a table.

"What the fuck? WHAT THE FUCK?!" Jesse yelled, horrified, looking over his own body in disbelief.

"Jimmy? Hey, hold it together, Jimmy!" Marlon said to Jimmy, who looked dazed, whispering to himself "sat on all day... all day... all day..."

Something gargantuan moved in the distance, and the three friends felt vibrations come from the ground into their puny bodies, making their very skeletons vibrate.

Flip-flops. Or rather, it was the bare feet attached to the flip-flops that made the world tremble with every step. Normally, they would not be impressive; but the fact that they belonged to an 11-year-old boy was the single scariest thing in the world, to the three friends watching, slack-jawed, from the floor.

The boy had a shaved head, with the faintest fuzz that showed he'd have black hair if he let it grow. He wore an oversized white tank top, probably belonging to his older brother; it left nothing to the imagination, the neckhole falling to his sternum to show off the boy's toned body—that was the body of an outdoors kid who loved to be active. He put away the smartphone he was holding as soon as he noticed the shrinkees on the floor.

"Oh hey, mister robbers!" The boy said cheerfully. He had such a happy, easygoing smile that it was easy to forget he was pretty much Godzilla to them, both in size and lethality.

"We're not rob-" Marlon tried to say, but the boy cut him off, his voice so potent it washed away Marlon's pathetic little words.

"I'm so glad I put the house on that robbers' forum, we're getting two break-ins a week now!" The boy commented, looking overjoyed.

Then, it dawned on the three friends. Some households did this immoral but technically legal thing where they installed shrinking security in their home and used their underage children to "take out" the shrunken offenders. It was legal as long as a minor did it, after all.

"Sorry, I didn't introduce myself, how rude!" The giant boy said, seemingly remembering something. He stood as tall as his small frame allowed, and he flexed one bicep—there was a cute little mound, but no real muscle. "I'm Brett! I'm the great hero who beats up villains!" He stared down, and grinned widely. "Nice to meet you, villains!"

Jimmy burst forward, animated with despair, and he cried "Don't sit on me! Please! I-I can't take it!" His two friends tried to hold him back, but Jimmy threw himself to the ground and prostrated himself before Brett.

The flip-flop resting some paces away from the tinies suddenly moved, flew hiiiigh into the air, then stomped down with merciless strength. It was like a building collapsing on Jimmy, the young adult was fine one second and flattened to a red paste of exploded organs and blood the next. Marlon and Jesse could only stare, wide-eyed and terrified, as Brett casually stepped on their friend, a friendly smile on his face, as he replied "Sure!"

"J-Jimmy..." Marlon stuttered, staring at the sheer wall of the flip-flop in front of him. The last traces of Jimmy were a trickle of blood coming from underneath the sole, and a splatter of gore on Marlon's horrified face.

"Anyone got a preference? I take requests!" Brett happily offered.

"P-Please..." Jesse begged. "Don't kill us!"

Brett exploded into genuine, boyish laughter. "Nah, c'mon! Do ya wanna be eaten? I love the taste of you guys!"

"Wha- No, I don't-" Jesse tried to protest, but Brett simply bent down and snatched his torso between two fingers. Jesse was brought high, hiiiigh in the sky, until he could look down at the open mouth of the hungry boy. "WAIT! Feet!!"

Brett paused, seemingly surprised. "Huh?"

Jesse was in tears, now, but he kept talking. "F-feet! I have a foot fetish!! I-I don't wanna die..." Jesse sobbed then struggled to regain control of his voice. "I-I-I don't wanna die, but if... if I have to... I'd prefer feet..."

Brett beamed with happiness. "Awesome choice, bro! No reason I should be the only one havin' fun!" He casually kicked off one sandal, revealing the precise shape of his toes and sole imprinted on the insole. "Have fun!!"

It was such a kind sendoff as Brett lowered Jesse into the imprit of his heel that the tiny briefly thought Brett wouldn't actually kill him. The next moment, Brett braced himself against the doorframe, lifted his bare foot above the sandal, replacing the ceiling with a neverending expanse of boyish sole for Jesse. The foot came crashing down without warning or care; Brett then stamped his foot twice, blushing and grinning as he did so.

"Uwaaaaah! Steppin' on people really is the best!" Brett intoned, cheeks reddened and clearly having the time of his life. He then crouched next to the frozen, horrified Marlon, all smiles. "You know what my problem is? I'm a people pleaser. But I don't mind, I can make little guys like ya smile, so it's okay! So, what do you prefer?"

Marlon stared up, eyes recessed with fear and trauma. He could not flee, not cry, not talk; life seemed over already...

"Hey, speak up! Hmm... You know, I'm pretty hungry and I never got that snack soooooo..." Brett started saying, looking around like a shy boy hesitating to ask a girl to prom. "... How about I eatcha, yeah?!" He grinned widely, looking hopefuly. "I'll make the trip fun, I swear!"

A single tear rolled down Marlon's cheek. "Is... is that what you did to Kyle? You ate him...?"

"Uh? Whatchy talkin' about?" Brett asked, confused.

"You... we... Kyle was shrunk and..."

"Oh yeah, I kinda stepped on his girlfriend hahaha!" Brett laughed innocently, as if remembering a gag or something from a cartoon. "But nah, Kyle's fine. How do ya know my bro?"

Marlon stood there, feeling nothing other than dread, and loss. Kyle was... safe? Jesse and Jimmy... died for nothing? "We... are his best friends..." Marlon responded, his head feeling as if it was about to explode.

"Oh shi-" Brett slapped one hand on his mouth, as if scared to be caught saying a grown-up word, even as the guts of two grownups decorated his soles. "I mean, geez, you could have said that before, guy." Brett sighed and scratched the back of his head. Then, he stood tall and glared down at Marlon. "You could seriously get me in trouble, you know that? That's really not nice!"

Before Marlon could retort anything about Brett crushing Jimmy without asking for information, the boy leaned down and snatched him up. The first thought in the young adult's mind was that he was about to be eaten, to silence him. Brett dangled him by the legs, head down and filling rapidly with blood, and shook him a few times, looking annoyed—but did not kill him. Instead, the boy started walking deeper into the house, keeping Marlon dangling at his side. The shrunken man couldn't help but notice that, with every step, Brett left a few red splotches on the ground—whatever little was left of Jimmy. Marlon wept.

"Kyyyyyyle, I got a surprise for ya!" Brett announced as he entered his bedroom and closed the door behind him.

On the boy's desk was Kyle, looking tiny even next to Brett's school supplies—even an eraser could be a whole couch for the shrunken man.

"If it's not Lissa back alive and well, I don't want your fucking surprise," Kyle growled in response.

Brett pouted in reaction, puffing his cheeks. "C'mon, stop being so childish! Move on already! I got you someone to play with!"

"Someone to-" Kyle's eyes widened in horror when he saw Marlon, nearly passed out from being dangled around for so long. Brett dropped Marlon roughly on the desk and sat at his chair, his torso towering far above his two shrinkees, a wide smile on his face.

"Marlon! Marlon, are you okay?!" Kyle begged, kneeling next to his friend. Marlon groaned and weakly moved, but did not answer. "FUCK! Brett, did you step on him too?!" Kyle angrily asked his little brother. Brett lost his smile.

"HEY! Why are you accusin' me! I'm innocent!" Brett argued, and he slammed a palm on the desk, near his brother and Marlon. "It's unfair, you can't always bully me like that!"

Kyle gulped, looking at the hand that could have effortlessly pulped him to nothing, and he swallowed back his pride. He replied with his head low. "You... you're right, I'm sorry... Of course you didn't step on my friend, it was an accident when you crushed Lissa... I'm such an idiot..."

"I mean..." Brett's lips briefly tucked into a big smile, as if reminiscing about a beautiful memory, before he suppressed it and tried to look serious. "I did not step on THIS friend..."

"Brett...?" Kyle asked, feeling dread rise again.

Marlon stirred awake and grabbed Kyle's collar at that moment. "Jimmy... Jesse... the... the boy... he... stepped on them..." The words were faint, but they resonated like a gong.

"He did... what?"

Brett shrugged. "I mean, they tried to break through the-ha... hahahHAHAHA!" Brett exploded into loud, inappropriate laughter, wiggling on his chair and throwing his head back to laugh as powerfully as his lungs allowed. "Sorry! Hahaha, so-sorry! I mean, I stepped on two guys today, but I guess I only killed one!"

Brett swung his sandaled foot atop the desk. Not only did its heel come dangerously close to taking out Kyle and Marlon, the action gave the two shrinkees a peerless view of the underside of the boy's flip-flop. The remains of several adults were scattered all over the sole, a mixture of blood, guts and ripped fabric; much of it was squished together into tight bundles of crushed flesh in the treads of the sandal, though a lot had been flattened and decorated the sole itself. Lissa and Jimmy were both among the freshest bits, but there were others... so many others...

"I had no idea... how could I live with Brett and have no idea...?" Kyle asked no-one, driven to his knees in awe of the new perspective he gained on the joyful little boy who shared his life.

The giant boy, unbothered, pushed one finger between his bare heel and the insole of the sandal. There, it wiggled for a few moments until something was audibly peeled off and fell heavily onto the wood of the boy's desk.

"Jesse...? JESSE!" Kyle cried, disbelieving what his eyes showed him. His friend was discheveled, covered in blood and looking disoriented, but he was alive.

"I really liked what ya did down there," Brett commented, retracting his foot, which landed back on the floor below with a distinct boom. "You were lickin' so good!"

Ignoring Brett, Kyle rushed to Jesse's side to check on him. Jesse was still abundantly bleeding from a severe-looking head wound—Kyle offered his shirt to cover it, leaving him barechested.

"He needs a doctor! NOW!" Kyle screamed at Brett, who hummed to himself.

"I wonder if I can get a doctor to try entering the house when mom and dad aren't home..." Brett pondered out loud.

"What?! Not shrinking a doctor! Jesse needs to go to the hospital!"

Brett's grin widened a little bit. "BuuuuUUUuuttt, if I bring Jesse to the hospital, the adults will take him away from me."

Kyle couldn't believe what he heard. "Jesse is not yours! What are you going on abou-"

"Wrong! He's mine, dad said so!" Brett countered with a snort. "Every person shrunk by the house is mine! Next, I'll get my best friend from school to visit and ZAP! Mine too!" Brett looked giddy at the very thought, blushing and closing his eyes in yearning. "Don't worry, you won't be alone long!"

"That's wrong! Brett!" Kyle screamed, losing his mind. His brother stared at him for a moment, as if pondering what to do, when Marlon reprised the cry of revolt: "I'll never be yours, you evil little annoying shi-" Brett's fist, slamming onto the desk—and Marlon—at full speed, exploded the shrinkee into a red puree that covered Kyle and Jesse from head to toe, leaving both stunned.

Brett did not look... _angry_. Not particularly. Mildly annoyed, at most.

"Y'know Kyle, I like you! You're a cool big bro!" Brett said, as if the man he'd just killed hadn't mattered at all. "But if you're gonna be a downer, you're not gonna get, like, a special treatment y'know?"

Kyle stared at the coiled fist standing where Marlon had been alive a moment ago, then back up at his "little" brother. Tears started pouring out of his eyes, then. By his side, Jesse prostrated himself deeply towards Brett. "I will serve you for as long as you wish, Master Brett!"

The giant boy blushed and grinned at the nickname. "I'm so glad I gotcha too, Jesse! I'm the great hero who beats up villains, so as long as you're good, you're not gettin' beaten up!" Brett petted Jesse's head, uncaring about rubbing his massive fingertip into the tiny's cracked skull. "So... You, uh, you think you could teach Kyle how to lick my feet all proper-like?"

That jolted Kyle awake from his stupor. He was about to scream his refusal to obey when Brett grabbed a handkerchief, scrubbed Marlon's pulverized remains off his hand and casually threw it into the bin by his desk. Lips trembling, Kyle nodded slowly, sullenly. Brett's sunniest smile answered his brother's abdication.
