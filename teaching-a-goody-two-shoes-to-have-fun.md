## Summary

James stared at the TV, horrified, as he was most days when watching the news. Ever since the shrinking virus swept the nation, there were horror shows in the media all the time; a son crushing his mother underfoot after returning from school not knowing she caught the virus, a babysitter eaten alive by the boy he was looking over when he suddenly shrank, countless innocents trampled to death because they'd contracted the virus in the middle of a mall or a school hallway.

As a result, the mistreatment of shrinkees could not be punished severely by the law, Congress ruled, otherwise countless innocent—and full-sized, meaning productive and capable of paying taxes—people would go to prison for "merely" taking out a few shrinkees.

Recently, an underground ring of shrinkee traffickers had been exposed in the same city he lived in; to everyone's surprise, the main traffickers were a group of schoolchildren bringing their shrunken siblings, sometimes even their parents, to school, and trading them. Because the boys in that school had contacts with adults who sold shrinkees for profit under the table, a lot of these kids' relatives and friends ended up sold to strangers, given the worst imaginable fate. The lucky ones were enslaved, exploited and humiliated daily—James did not want to think about the kids who traded their shrunken father or mother for a bag of marbles, unknowingly sending their loved one to a gruesome death for some sicko's pleasure. James stared at the screen as the judge announced "Not guilty" and banged his gavel. The defendants, a bunch of elementary-age school kids, loudly cheered.

Hugo went to that school, James pondered. Hugo was the kindest, softest-hearted kid in the whole world, he worried about stepping on ladybugs and James had even seen him cry his heart out after accidentally sitting on an ant. Of course Hugo had no idea such human trafficking was happening among his classmates, but were any of them his friends? James' eyes swept across the celebrating defendants on TV. Did any of them offer Hugo to trade a tiny for his lunch or something?

No, James decided. Hugo would have told him everything if that had been the case. Despite being a shrinkee too, James had never truly felt like he was less than a person. Other than climbing furniture—and even then, Hugo typically helped him by carrying him everywhere—James' life had hardly changed since the shrinking. Hugo couldn't possibly have been gentler with him than he already was.

The door handle clicked, pulling James out of his reverie. "Hugo! Welcome home!" He bounded to his feet atop the boy's bed and waved a hand at the opening door.

Hugo looked titanic to James, of course; he was twice the size of the Statue of Liberty, but moving, breathing and emitting an aura of warmth and life. In reality, Hugo was a runt even among 9-year-old kids, short and skinny as he was. He had shaggy brown hair and large, round glasses that made his eyes look big and innocent, and he was covered in freckles.

Unexpectedly, another kid walked in behind Hugo, making James freeze with surprise. The new boy had semi-long hair, dyed an unnaturally bright shade of red, and he was dressed all in black, tattered clothes, with black nailpolish and an eyebrow piercing. The new boy's gargantuan, bulky sneakers stomped heavily on the wooden floor, unlike Hugo's soft, careful steps. The giant redhead's eyes seemed to be scanning the room; or rather, the floor.

"Who's-" James' question was violently interrupted when the redhead sat on the bed, or rather threw himself down at it, ass first. James cried out in shock and fear and tried to leap out of the way. He was only semi-successful; the butt narrowly missed compressing him down into the sheets, but the weight was so immense that the entire landscape sagged beneath it, making James helpless collapse into the canyon, towards the newcomer's butt.

His descent came to a painful stop as he collided, face-first, into the sheer wall of denim that covered the boy's asscheek, and he collapsed on his back onto the sheets. More fear than pain, thankfully, James thought. In the distance, he could hear his name be called by Hugo, who seemed to be looking around the room for him. Before he could holler to be noticed, however, the redhead's ass lifted suddenly. Not enough to mean to stand up, just a couple inches, forcing James to roll deeper, into the chasm that the ass had formed a moment ago.

"H-hey, James?" Hugo called, not two feet away, but with his back turned. "Show yourself, my friend's not dangerous, I promise!" At the exact same moment, the ass collapsed with every pound behind the 9-year-old giant's frame packed into it.

James genuinely thought he had become flat, until the pain stopped making his head hurt quite so much. The boy's ass was flush was the fabric of the bedsheets, and James' entire body was so tightly compressed that every atom in his body was screaming for mercy, but because both surfaces were soft, they deformed just enough to let him survive the impact. But not enough to breathe.

In the distance, muffled as if coming from a previous life, his little brother's voice kept calling him in an increasingly-worried tone, while James felt his lungs burn while the greatest weight he could have ever imagined pressed down on him.

Mercy. He couldn't speak the word, with no air left in him, but he thought it really hard. If there was a god out there, James was begging it for mercy.

Hugo was that god, James realized a second later—Hugo's panicked voice exploded around him: "You're sitting on him!" as the weight all disappeared at once and light flooded the place. His little brother had grabbed the new kid by the arm and yanked him to his feet.

"James, are you okay? Uh...? James?" Hugo asked, increasingly confused. Kneeling next to the bed, he was inspecting the dent made by his friend's butt, but no sigh of his brother.

James, despite himself, was embedded into the buttcheek of the boy, who was now standing behind Hugo. The idea of getting unstuck and falling to his death was terrifying for James—so he felt profound dread when he saw the fingers of the newcomer aimed directly at him. He expected the giant to scratch the itch James' body was surely causing. Instead, the fingers wrapped around his legs and peeled him off before bringing him high, much higher in the sky, at eye level with the redhead.

"Is that what you're looking for?" The boy asked, laughter in his voice, as he stared closely at the terrified shrinkee. His tongue wetted his lips briefly.

"Oh gosh James, you're okay! You worried me," Hugo said, sighing with relief.

James wouldn't exactly call himself "okay" after being sat on, and while being dangled upside-down by a boy he did not trust, but he was breathing and had no broken bones, at least. But those ebony eyes staring at his soul...

"Bastian, can you give me my brother, now...?" Hugo asked politely, one extended palm offered to deposit James in. James wished that, for once, his brother could be assertive.

"Hm..? Why? He could join our games," the redhead, Bastian, countered.

Hugo seemed confused. "You said we're going to play video games... James is too small to hold a controller."

Bastian smirked. "I know. He could be the prize."

"Wha- I'm not giving you my brother!" Hugo protested, and he agitated his upturned palm a bit.

Bastian did not hand James over. "It's not giving, if I earn him by kicking your ass!"

James was certain of it, now. This Bastian was bad news. Bad, very bad news. As soon as he went home, James would need to have a serious heart-to-heart with Hugo. "H-He said no!" James argued towards the building-sized face of the punk holding him.

Bastian snorted, amused. "Right. Whatevs."

James allowed himself to breathe again after he was carelessly tossed atop his brother's palm. Hugo seemed relieved too, as if he truly considered that Bastian might have never let James go. The shrinkee was not used to crying, but he had to swallow back a few tears for the first time since he shrank.

Hugo apologized to him over and over as he deposited him to the ground with instructions to go into his little hidey home under Hugo's bed. To James' relief, Hugo kept a close eye on him until he was just under the awning of the bed.

"O-oh, Bastian! No shoes in the bedroom, I-I forgot," Hugo said after turning his attention back to his guest.

Before walking deeper under the bed, towards the safety of his little toy home too far away for any boy to reach it without moving the whole bed, James looked back towards the titans dominating the landscape of the child's bedroom.

Bastian's eyes met James'. Bastian smiled and lifted his eyebrows briefly while, far below, he pulled one bare foot out of its sneaker before the other. James immediately thought there was something crass with Bastian not wearing socks. His toes also had black nailpolish, although the polish on one foot, and only one foot, was degraded, as if something repeatedly touched Bastian's toenails.

James made sure Bastian was sitting down and playing the game before he took a few steps towards the sideturned shoe.

"Heeeelp!" It was definitely coming from inside. James felt dread like never before.

"Is... is anyone here?" James called into the gaping maw of the sneaker. There was a warmth in the air, like Bastian's presence, so overwhelming that it lingered in the air even after removing his shoe.

A response came to him in the form of a half-laugh, half-sob. "Here!" the man said. "Please!"

It was too dark for James to discern anything. The shrinkee hesitated, then took a few steps away to get a look at the boys playing Smash. Both were focused on the game, backs turned to James. He tried to gather his courage. Behind the fabric of the shoe, he could faintly hear the cries of the poor victim Bastian kept in his shoe.

When James passed the threshold of Bastian's shoe, taking one step inside, when the room's ceiling was replaced with the insides of the sneaker, his prey instincts cried as loud as possible. He felt like a mouse taking its first step onto the tongue of a slumbering lion.
