Title: Time turned-on
Author: Giantboyslit
Source: https://archiveofourown.org/works/86731126
Tags: Macro/Micro, Shrinking, Shotacon, Giant Boy, Mad Science, Crush Fetish, Foot Fetish, Feet, Stolen Identity, Tinies belong under boys' feet

Summary:
In a world where only kids are vaccinated against the shrinking virus, one boy does not appreciate how much of a gift it is. He bemoans how small and shrimpy he is, without realizing he's basically a walking, breathing God to the shrinkees beneath him.

==================================================

"Seriouslyyyyy?" Andrew whined at the librarian.

She gave him a sorry smile. "This is the adults-only section, sorry little guy! We have kid-friendly books over in that section, they are more fitting for someone your age," she offered, pointing towards an area with little benches and decorations made of rainbows and cartoon bees.

Andrew scowled. All the cool comic books with blood and action were in the adults section! He didn't want to read about a hungry caterpillar or whatever. Adults had no clue what it was like to be a kid, looked down upon by everyone, never taken seriously-

"Now?! It cannot be now!" The librarian suddenly cried out. She grabbed her purse and started running towards the door. "The doctor said- I'm supposed to have two more days!!" She tripped over her own feet and collapsed onto the library's floor, fear etched onto her face. "HELP!" She cried in Andrew's direction, one hand extended towards him.

Andrew stared as the adult woman started diminishing in stature. Her clothes remained the same size, so she was starting to swim in the fabric. Her movements were getting increasingly frantic and terrified the smaller she was getting.

Seeing an adult shrink was always a great sight. Andrew had only seen it six, maybe seven times. The adults always acted like such drama queens, he thought. It wasn't that big a deal. Andrew and everyone his age had long been vaccinated against the shrinking virus, but the vaccine was ineffective in most adults, so to the boy, it was just a funny thing that happened to others and he never needed to worry about.

The librarian was less than two inches tall, now. She was naked, crawling on the floor like some gross bug. Andrew scowled, remembering how she refused to let him read comic books earlier. He started walking towards the exit of the library, but he deviated slightly from his path, just enough to line up his sneaker caaaaarefully and-STOMP!

He expected a red splatter. When the boys toyed with shrinkees at school, they usually got them to pop with just one step. Andrew felt his cheeks redden with shame at the thought that, because of his short and skinny frame, he was standing fully on top of that shrinkee and he could still feel her desperate struggles through the rubber insole of his shoe. Andrew really couldn't do anything right.

Shamefully, he lifted his other foot off the ground and balanced himself entirely on one leg, like a crane, putting every bit of his body weight—plus the weight of his school backpack—onto the tiny librarian. It was a slow process; Andrew felt his sole lower incrementally before, finally, becoming flush with the floor. When he lifted his sneaker a moment later, the shrinkee was definitely smashed and bloody, but she was mostly whole.

Andrew made a grossed-out face as he scraped her off his shoe using a nearby shelf's edge. "Ew. Fuckin' ew."

Andrew walked out of the library, still ruminating on his dark thoughts, when he bumped into a tall, a very tall man with a square jaw and a scruffy chin. The man looked like he was in a hurry of some sort.

"A... a kid? Haha, I found one!" The man said to himself, grinning awkwardly at Andrew, who eyed him suspiciously in return. "Sorry, my apologies! My name's Professor Hagara, but you can call me Prof!"

Andrew pondered that this man did look like some kind of professor. He was so tall that he surely stood out in any crowd, he had glasses and broad shoulders. Idly, he thought that nobody in the world stopped that man from reading even the goriest comic books. Lucky bastard.

"I was... I was looking for a test subject for my new invention, you see!" Prof explained. "A body-transfer machine, which makes two people invert bodies for one day. I was thinking that maybe a neighborhood kid might want to know what it's like to be a respected adult man for a day, hm? I have adults working under me, you know? Adults who would call you sir and respect you, if you are in my body!"

The professor's tone was cracking with anxiety and hope to be told "yes," and any adult would have seen it as strange; Andrew, with all the gullibility of a kid who thinks he knows it all, saw nothing.

"No shit?! I could be old like you?" Andrew asked, stars in his eyes. "But... why would you wanna be small and weak and fragile like me...?" He looked down at his own body, which barely came up to the professor's belly-button, sullen.

"Small, weak and fragile...?" Prof repeated the words, sounding pensive. "No, your body is much greater, bigger and powerful than you understand." He placed a hand on Andrew's shoulder. "Why don't we give it a try? But..." Prof looked anxiously at his watch. "... we need to do it fast."

\*\*\*

"C'mon, is it ready?" Andrew asked impatiently.

The professor was typing rapidly on his computer's keyboard while the boy waited in some egg-shaped pod in the man's laboratory. "I assure you that I'm more impatient about this than you are," Prof retorted, and he checked his wristwatch again before grimacing. "Well, time's up, so this will have to do."

He got up and shoved himself into a twin pod, next to Andrew's, before using some sort of remote to activate the device.

"So, when this is done, I can go in the adults section of the library, right?" Andrew asked for the Nth time today. "I can even watch horror movies alone?!"

"Yeah, yeah, all of that and more," Prof promised, and he leaned back into the pod.

A flash of light followed, then two gasps, one from a deep, manly voice and the other in the soft and airy tone of an elementary-school boy. "It worked!" They both exclaimed in unison.

"Wow, you really are small!" Andrew mocked Prof. Although, in a way, he was insulting himself. Standing over 6ft6, Andrew felt like the king of the world. He shoved his now wide, slightly-hairy hand on top of the little boy's head and roughly messed up his hair. "Hard not to bully you at this size, pipsqueak!"

The professor, now looking like your average schoolboy, had a very non-childlike smirk. "I wouldn't do that if I were you."

"Why? I can bully pipsqueaks if I wan- Uh?" The watch around his wrist beeped quite loudly. "What's that?"

Prof put his hands in his short pockets and smirked up at Andrew. "The alarm warning me that the shrinking virus is about to take effect. Glad I got you to trade bodies in time."

"YOU LITTLE-" Andrew grabbed his own body by the collar and lifted him off the floor... for a moment. Every second, he could feel size melting off his new body, and with it, strength. His arm strained for a moment before he was forced to set the professor back down on the floor. They were almost seeing eye-to-eye, now.

For the first time in as long as he could remember, Andrew felt fear. The adult clothes on his back were now so baggy that they threatened to consume him, and he was losing inches every second. What was, a few moments before, a short and skinny kid he could mess with, was now taller than him.

Andrew shoved the rapidly-growing clothes off him and tried to run, now naked as a newborn. The other Andrew—the impostor—just had to take one step forward to crash Andrew's own worn, ratty sneaker into the real Andrew's path. If the shrinkee could still see above the shoe, he could just barely do so, and his size kept being robbed from him. "Please! Let me go!"

"Uuuh? But didn't you want to be an adult? To not be... what were the words...?" The giant child pretended to ponder. "Oh yeah... to not be weak, small and fragile anymore? What, you don't like being older?"

Andrew had felt weird since the body-swap, even before shrinking. He got it, now. "Being older sucks!! My- I mean, your arms are slow and achy, and I can't run fast, and I feel unbalanced!"

"Yeah, welcome to middle age," Prof laughed from above. He looked appreciatively over his own new body, even pulling up his shirt to run an appreciative palm over Andrew's bare torso. "I'm really happy with the trade, honestly. You have such a toned and youthful body! I'm going to enjoy it more than you ever would have."

Andrew was now so small that he was at eye level with the sole of the bulky sneaker blocking his path. Thankfully, the shrinking had finally stopped. Looking up, he wondered how he could ever have felt small and powerless. Now that he could see his own body from a new perspective, he was in awe of how massive, how intimidating his body was. He was like fucking Godzilla! But with a toned torso and honestly a really cute face! Who cared if he was a little late on his growth?!

"I'll get my body back tomorrow!" Andrew yelled up, as a threat. "And then, I'll get my hands on tiny you, and you'll see!"

The giant boy puffed with laughter. "Really? You think you're gonna survive long enough for that?" He crouched, turning his crotch into Andrew's sky. "Just so you know, the body transfer becomes permanent if one of us dies before the 24 hours are up." And he grinned, almost innocently. "Sorry, I promise I'll live your life to the fullest!"

Andrew only started the realize the position he was in. His eyes swept the empty lab. Nobody knew he was here. With a quiet, dawning horror, he remembered the librarian lady. Was THAT what she'd felt when Andrew had stepped on her just because of a bad mood—no, worse, a temporary annoyance?

"P... Please...?" Andrew asked of the boy god towering over him—he kept trying to forget that he was, himself, said boy god, but when the face he usually saw in the mirror gave him a sad smile, he couldn't ignore that his body had truly been stolen.

"Listen, kid. There's two paths ahead for us," the professor explained. "One, I kill you here and now, and I get to live life again from the start, do it right. Two, you live and our bodies swap back tomorrow, leaving me stranded as a tiny with an angry kid who wants to harm me. You get it, right?"

Andrew's cheeks were wet with tears as he pleaded. "I'll take care of you! I-I'll get mom to buy you a terrarium, and you can be my pet and live in my room! I'll feed you good food, and nobody will step on you, promise!"

The giant stood back up to his fullest height. Fuck, how could Andrew ever have considered himself a runt? Seeing himself just standing there made his head spin with vertigo.

"Appealing offer, but nah. You die, sorry," the giant kid said with a big ol' smile on his face. Then, he pressed onto the heel of his left sneaker with the toes of the right one and yanked one socked foot free. "Oof! When was the last time you showered, little guy?" The gargantuan little boy lifted the socked foot closed to his face and made a grimace. "Sheesh! Well, you chose your own fate, I guess," and he ripped the sock off in one swift move, pelting Andrew below with sock lint.

Seeing—and smelling—his own bare foot hovering in the sky above his head like the sword of judgement made Andrew cry for his mommy. It did not stop the sole slamming down on him without mercy.

Andrew's consciousness blinked in and out a few times. When he really woke up, his entire front was embedded into the singlest heaviest thing in existence, or so it felt, weighing down on him. It was soft... extremely pungent, and it enveloped Andrew whole.

"Geez, still alive?" Came his own voice, magnified a thousandfold. That's when Andrew remembered he'd just been stepped on, and he panicked. He knew he wasn't heavy enough to one-shot tinies by stepping on them, but Prof did not know!

The foot lifted into the air... and to Andrew's horror, he lifted alongside it, glued to the underside of his own sole. Suddenly, the course reversed, and the foot stomped hard into the tiled floor. Andrew felt pain like he couldn't imagine, but he was still alive; bleeding, concussed but alive.

"Listen kid, I'm not a monster," Prof said after observing he was still breathing. "Plus, I don't enjoy breaking my own body, you know? I just need you to stop breathing, okay buddy?"

And with that, the boy's fingers grabbed Andrew's ankle and peeled him painfully off the bare sole... and shoved him between the first and second toe! His screams of mercy accomplished nothing—the toes squeezed together, and all air left Andrew's lungs.

It was a long, agonizing death. Andrew's mind, in the last few moments, was filled with regrets. He regretted not messing with more shrinkees, regretted not being more patient with growing up, regretted not enjoying how powerful his body already was...

His life was snuffed out at last, and the professor's shrunken body was tossed into a nearby trash can with a sigh. When Andrew made it home that evening, he seemed to not remember most things, but his parents patiently helped him get used to his bedroom and his routine again. Anything for their little angel.
